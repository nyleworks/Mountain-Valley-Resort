About the resort

Unpretentious luxury at the foot of Kinabalu

Renovated in 2019, Mountain Valley Resort sits on a small hill at Tenompok, the gateway to the picturesque town of Bundu Tuhan. Surrounded by lush greenery, it offers bright, breezy rooms with views of majestic Mount Kinabalu and the Bundu Tuhan valleys. It's a cool, quiet escape from the heat of Kota Kinabalu.

It is the area's first eco-tourism project, built to support the Bundu Tuhan community. The village's native forest reserve, protected under the tagal system, is recognised as one of Sabah's best-managed Indigenous and Community Conserved Areas.
